Welcome to Kandy

A city of temples, hills and royal history

Set among forested hills in Sri Lanka’s Central Province, Kandy was the last capital of the island’s kings. Today its lakeside centre, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and the surrounding countryside make it one of the country’s most rewarding places to explore.

In a single day you can combine sacred sites, colonial architecture, botanical gardens and sweeping viewpoints — the trick is choosing an order that suits you.
