The city

Kandy, the hill capital

Kandy sits in the hills of Sri Lanka’s Central Province, wrapped around a tranquil lake and surrounded by forest, tea country and the Mahaweli River. It was the seat of the last Sinhalese kingdom, and its royal and religious heritage is recognised in the Sacred City of Kandy UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic draws pilgrims from across the island, while botanical gardens, colonial-era buildings, medieval temples and hilltop viewpoints give visitors plenty to see within a short distance of the city centre.
